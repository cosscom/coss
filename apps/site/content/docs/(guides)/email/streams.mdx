---
title: Transactional and marketing email
description: Two streams, so marketing complaints never hold up password resets.
---

coss.com sends email in two streams. **Transactional** email is what a user expects because of
something they did: sign-in links, receipts, password resets. **Marketing** email is what you send
to many people at once: newsletters, announcements, [broadcasts](/docs/email/broadcasts).

Each stream has its own sending configuration and its own reputation. Marketing mail gets more
complaints and bounces than transactional mail; keeping them apart means a newsletter that annoys
people can pause marketing email, never your password resets. With [dedicated
IPs](/docs/email/dedicated-ips), both streams leave from your organization's own IPs, still each
with its own configuration and reputation.

## Sending marketing email

Broadcasts are always marketing email. To send a single marketing email yourself, pass
`stream: "marketing"`:

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
await coss.email.send({
  from: "Acme <news@acme.com>",
  to: "ada@example.com",
  subject: "You're invited",
  html: "<p>Join us on Thursday.</p>",
  stream: "marketing",
  category: "events",
});
```

A marketing email goes to one recipient, needs your postal address in the marketing settings, and
may not set `List-Unsubscribe` headers itself: coss.com adds them, with an unsubscribe link and your
address (see [topics and preferences](/docs/email/topics-and-preferences)). Contacts who
unsubscribed, or opted out of the email's topic (its `category`), are skipped; addresses that aren't
contacts are sent to (you vouch for their consent).

Transactional email ignores marketing consent: someone who unsubscribed from your newsletter still
gets their receipts. Only an explicit opt-out of the topic matching a transactional email's
`category` skips it.

## Reputation

coss.com measures bounce and complaint rates per stream. When marketing email crosses its thresholds,
**marketing email is paused** for the organization (an `account.marketing_paused` webhook and an
email to owners and admins), and transactional email keeps going. A complaint about a marketing
email suppresses that address for marketing email only; a permanent bounce suppresses it for
everything. A pause is lifted by coss.com support after a review; [delivery](/docs/email/delivery)
covers what happens to transactional email.
