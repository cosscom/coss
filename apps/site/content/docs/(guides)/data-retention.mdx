COSS keeps message data only as long as it is useful for delivery, debugging and abuse
prevention. A background job deletes data once it passes the period below, for every
organization and environment (sandbox and live alike). Deletion is permanent: COSS cannot restore
deleted data.

<RetentionTable />

Periods start when the data is created (for example, when you call `coss.email.send()`), unless
the table says otherwise. Deletion runs continuously, so data may disappear shortly after its
period ends, never before.

## Emails

An email is deleted <RetentionPeriod of="emails" /> after it was created, together with
everything recorded about it: recipients and their statuses, delivery events, opens and clicks,
tracked links and attachment names. Emails that are still queued or scheduled are kept until they
are sent or canceled.

After deletion:

- `coss.email.get()`, `coss.email.listEvents()` and the dashboard return `not_found` for it.
- If it had open or click tracking, its tracked links stop redirecting and its open pixel stops
  counting.
- Suppressions created by its bounces and complaints stay in place: they still name the email's
  id, which no longer resolves.

Attachment content is discarded much earlier: as soon as the email is sent, fails or is
canceled. Only each attachment's name, type, size and checksum stay with the email.

## Received emails

A [received email](/docs/email/receiving) is deleted <RetentionPeriod of="received_emails" />
after it arrived, with its raw message, headers, bodies and attachments. Delete one sooner with
`coss.email.received.delete()` or from its dashboard page: its content goes right away. Messages
blocked for a virus never have their content stored.

## Webhook deliveries

Every event sent to your [webhook endpoints](/docs/webhooks), with its delivery status and the
log of attempts (status codes, timing and up to 1 KB of each response), is deleted
<RetentionPeriod of="webhook_deliveries" /> after the event. A delivery that is still being
retried is kept until it succeeds or gives up.

## Keeping your own records

To keep message history longer, store it on your side as it happens: subscribe a webhook endpoint
to the events you need (`email.sent`, `email.delivered`, `email.bounced`, …) and record them in
your own database. Event payloads include the email's id, sender, recipients, subject, tags and
category.

## What is never deleted automatically

- **Suppressions** stay until you remove them, so an address that bounced or complained stays
  protected however long ago that was. See [suppressions](/docs/email/suppressions).
- **Account and configuration data**: your organization, its members, projects, environments,
  API keys, domains, webhook endpoints and settings. Anything you delete yourself (a key, a
  domain, an endpoint) is removed right away.

## Abuse prevention

COSS screens a sample of live messages for abuse and keeps the verdict (never the content) for
<RetentionPeriod of="content_screenings" />. Your account standing's history (warnings,
suspensions and reinstatements) is kept for <RetentionPeriod of="reputation_history" />, and the
latest change is kept for as long as the organization exists.
