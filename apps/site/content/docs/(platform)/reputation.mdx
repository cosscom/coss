Your organization has one **standing**, measured from what its live environments send:

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                  |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `good`      | Every check is within its thresholds                                                     |
| `warning`   | A check crossed its warning threshold. Nothing is refused; fix the cause before it grows |
| `suspended` | Live sending is refused until coss.com support reinstates the organization               |

Sandbox environments are never measured, and keep working while an organization is suspended.

## What is measured

Rates are counted per recipient over a rolling window of <ReputationWindow /> (never reaching back
before the organization's last reinstatement). Suppressed, failed, queued and canceled recipients
don't count, and neither do the [mailbox simulator's](/docs/environments#sandbox-behaviour)
addresses. A rate is only acted on once the window holds enough volume to make it meaningful; below
that, it is reported but never warns. The default thresholds:

<ReputationThresholds />

These are stricter than most mailbox providers' own, so coss.com acts before they do. coss.com
can set different thresholds for an organization: [`GET /v1/reputation`](#reading-your-standing)
returns the ones that apply to yours, check by check.

- **Email** is measured per [stream](/docs/email/streams#reputation). Transactional bounces and
  complaints decide the standing. Marketing bounces and complaints never suspend the organization:
  at their higher threshold **marketing email is paused** (broadcasts and `stream: "marketing"`
  sends are refused with `403` `marketing_paused`) while transactional email keeps going.
- **SMS** is measured by the share of numbers that replied STOP and of messages carriers filtered
  as spam (see [opt-outs and reputation](/docs/sms/receiving#opt-outs-and-reputation)).
- **Hooks** deliveries that failed every attempt only warn: they usually mean your customers'
  endpoints no longer exist (see [Hooks delivery](/docs/hooks/delivery#reputation)).
- **Content screening** blocks a message it is confident is abusive and suspends live sending at
  once; messages it flags warn. See [content screening](/docs/content-screening).
- A [sub-account](/docs/email/subaccounts#reputation) has its own standing: its rates pause that
  sub-account alone, while your organization's standing still counts everything it sends.

coss.com re-evaluates an organization as soon as a new live bounce or complaint arrives, and every
hour while it is warned. A warning clears by itself once every check is back within its thresholds.

## When live sending is suspended

- Live emails and SMS are refused with `403` `permission_error` (`account_suspended`). Sends coss.com
  had already accepted may still go out; scheduled sends wait until the suspension is lifted.
- New live API keys can't be created. Sandbox keys and environments keep working, and so does
  everything that isn't a send.
- **A suspension is never lifted automatically.** Contact coss.com support: a person reviews the
  account and reinstates it, and rates are measured afresh from then on. A marketing pause works the
  same way.

## Notifications

Every change of standing is sent to the `account.*` [webhooks](/docs/webhooks#organization-events)
of each live environment:

| Type                           | Sent when                                     |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| `account.reputation_warning`   | A check crossed its warning threshold         |
| `account.reputation_recovered` | The warning cleared                           |
| `account.suspended`            | Live sending was suspended                    |
| `account.reinstated`           | coss.com support lifted the suspension        |
| `account.marketing_paused`     | Marketing email was paused                    |
| `account.marketing_reinstated` | coss.com support lifted the marketing pause   |

Warnings, suspensions and marketing pauses are also emailed to the organization's owners and admins
(those with a verified address), with the checks behind them and what to do. Recoveries and
reinstatements aren't emailed. The dashboard shows a banner, and its **Reputation** page lists the
checks and the history of the standing.

## Reading your standing

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
const { data: reputation } = await coss.reputation.get();

if (reputation && reputation.status !== "good") {
  for (const check of reputation.checks) {
    if (check.severity !== "ok") console.log(check.trigger, check.rate, check.message);
  }
}
if (reputation?.marketing.status === "paused") {
  console.log("Marketing email is paused:", reputation.marketing.reason);
}
```

Any key with `reputation:read` reads it ([`GET /v1/reputation`](/docs/api-reference/reputation/get-reputation)).
Each check carries its `rate`, `count` and `volume` over the `window`, and the `warning_threshold`,
`suspend_threshold` and `minimum_volume` that apply to your organization.

## Keeping a good standing

- Send only to people who asked for your messages, and remove addresses that bounce: coss.com
  [suppresses](/docs/email/suppressions) them for you after a permanent bounce or a complaint.
- Make unsubscribing easy: marketing email gets [one-click unsubscribe](/docs/email/unsubscribe).
- Keep marketing on the marketing stream, so a campaign's complaints never hold up password resets.
- Test with a sandbox key and the simulator's addresses, never by sending to made-up addresses from
  a live environment.

The [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use#3-bounce-complaint-and-opt-out-rates) is the
policy behind these thresholds.
