Everyone who works in an organization is a **member** with one of three roles:

| Role       | Can                                                                                                                         |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything an admin can, plus: manage billing (plan, payment methods, add-ons) and [budgets](/docs/spend), require single sign-on, make or remove owners, delete the organization |
| **Admin**  | Use and configure every product in every project: domains, templates, webhooks, API keys, contacts, Risk rules, Hooks, SMS senders and so on. Invite and remove members and admins, change organization settings, set up [single sign-on](/docs/sso) and [directory sync](/docs/scim), read the [audit log](/docs/audit-log) and billing |
| **Member** | Read every project and environment in the dashboard (never webhook signing secrets), and create [personal API keys](/docs/environments#personal-keys) in the environments an owner or admin opened to them |

Members can't change anything in the dashboard: what they do, they do through their personal keys.
An organization has at least one owner at all times: the last owner can't leave, be removed or step
down until someone else is an owner.

The plan sets how many members an organization can have (Free: <Limit of="members" plan="free" />,
Pro: <Limit of="members" plan="pro" />, Scale: <Limit of="members" plan="scale" />; see
[plans and billing](/docs/billing)). One person can own up to 5 organizations, and belong to
any number.

## Invitations

Owners and admins invite people from the dashboard's **Members** page, as an admin or a member. Nobody is
invited as an owner: invite them, then make them an owner once they've joined.

- The invitation is emailed to the address you enter and expires after 48 hours; you can resend or
  cancel it until then. The invitee accepts it signed in with the invited address, once it's verified.
- Inviting needs a verified email address on your own account. Disposable addresses can't be invited.
- An organization has at most 50 pending invitations at a time, and invitations sent each day are
  rate limited.
- An invitation lasts only as long as its inviter may invite: when they leave, are removed or lose
  the admin role, their pending invitations are canceled.

With [directory sync](/docs/scim) your identity provider adds and removes members instead, and
people at your [SSO](/docs/sso) domains join as members the first time they sign in.

## Changing roles and removing members

Owners and admins change roles and remove members on the **Members** page. Only owners can
change an owner's role, remove an owner, or make someone an owner. Members added by directory sync
are managed in your identity provider: their role comes from its groups, and removing them there
removes them here (making one an owner is still possible in the dashboard).

Every change is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/audit-log). While an organization is suspended,
invitations and member changes are refused.

## API keys

Each API key belongs to one environment, and is either an **organization key** (servers, shared
jobs) or a **personal key** of one member, whose usage counts as that member's
[spend](/docs/spend):

- Owners and admins create both, and can assign a personal key to any member.
- Members create personal keys for themselves where an owner or admin has opened the environment to
  them. `coss login` and `coss init` create personal keys of the member who approves them.
- A personal key stops working when its owner leaves or is removed, and its organization-wide
  permissions (domains, billing, the audit log) follow its owner's current role.
- Every key has full access or a restricted set of permissions (sending only, read only, or the
  permissions you pick).

[Environments and API keys](/docs/environments#api-keys) has the details and the full permissions
table.

## Leaving an organization

Leave from the organization's **Settings** page (**Leave organization**). Your personal API keys in it are revoked, the
invitations you sent that are still pending are canceled, and you need a new invitation to come
back. The only owner has to make another member an owner first.

## Deleting an organization

Owners delete an organization from its **Settings** page (**Delete organization**), by typing its slug. Settle
any open invoices first, and a suspended organization can only be deleted by coss.com support.

Deletion takes effect at once: every API key and directory sync token stops working, the SSO
provider is removed, members and pending invitations are removed, scheduled emails never go out and
webhook deliveries stop. coss.com then deletes the organization's data in the background: its
projects and environments, emails, domains (also at the email provider), received mail, contacts,
logs, the audit log and everything else it holds. It can't be undone.

## Deleting your account

Delete your account from your account settings (**Delete account**). It deletes your profile, sign-in
methods (password, passkeys, connected Google or GitHub accounts, two-factor) and personal API keys,
signs you out everywhere and removes you from every organization. Organizations where you are the
only member are deleted with it.

It is refused while you are the only owner of an organization that has other members (make someone
else an owner, or delete the organization, first), while one of your organizations is suspended,
and while an organization that would be deleted has open invoices.
