HIPAA mode is one switch for your whole organization, for organizations that handle protected
health information (PHI). It narrows where PHI can go and who at coss.com can reach it. The
safeguards support your HIPAA compliance, which also depends on how you use coss.com.

<Callout type="warn">
Don't send PHI to coss.com without a signed Business Associate Agreement. Our
[Terms](/legal/terms) don't allow it.
</Callout>

## Turning it on

HIPAA mode is contract-only: it comes with an Enterprise contract and isn't available on self-serve
plans. You can't turn it on yourself, by any key or role. Ask for it from **Settings → Compliance**
in the dashboard. Then:

1. **You sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** with coss.com, and the mode becomes a line on
   your contract.
2. **Your contract must include the audit log**, as Enterprise does.
3. **coss.com staff turn it on** and record the BAA's date and reference. It applies at once,
   including to messages still waiting to go out.

Only coss.com staff can turn it off. Billing never does: a contract ending or a downgrade leaves the
mode on until someone decides otherwise. When it's turned off, requiring two-factor authentication
stays on until an owner changes it.

Every member can see the mode's status, your BAA and the vendors below on **Settings → Compliance**.
Your code can read it too, and so can agents, through the MCP server's `get_account_status`:

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
const { data: environment } = await coss.environment.get();
console.log(environment?.organization.hipaa_mode);
//                                     ^?
```

## What it changes

| Control                                | What it does                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AI screening off**                   | Your messages are never sampled or sent to an AI model ([content screening](/docs/content-screening)). |
| **AI classification off**              | Risk, Forms and Scheduling never send text to an AI model. Form submissions and assessments continue without content scores. |
| **Phone lookups off**                  | Live phone checks are refused, so numbers aren't sent to Twilio Lookup.                          |
| **Push notifications off**             | Your organization's and projects' names don't pass through Apple.                                |
| **SMS limited to US numbers**          | Live SMS goes to US numbers only, message bodies aren't kept after sending, and Verify codes need your own number. |
| **Email content discarded**            | Subjects, bodies, headers and template variables of live emails are deleted as soon as each email is finished. |
| **Open and click tracking off**        | Live emails have no tracking pixel or rewritten links, so link URLs aren't stored. Unsubscribe links still work. |
| **Request bodies not logged**          | API logs and log drains keep metadata such as the path and status, with no request or response bodies or query strings. |
| **Two-factor authentication required** | Every member needs two-factor authentication, or SSO through your own provider. Owners can't turn the requirement off. |
| **Automatic logoff**                   | Dashboard sessions end after 15 minutes without activity. Activity in organizations outside HIPAA mode doesn't count. |
| **coss.com staff can't view content**  | Staff can't reveal email content or impersonate your members, and internal email lists hide subjects and recipients. |
| **Encrypted delivery required**        | Email is sent only over TLS. A message is dropped rather than delivered unencrypted if the receiving server doesn't support it. |
| **Audit log kept**                     | Changes in your organization, including anything coss.com support does, are recorded and kept for as long as your account exists. |

Sandbox environments reach no vendor and keep test messages for developers to read, so the limits
on stored data, tracking and SMS apply to live environments only. Keep PHI out of them: they aren't
covered.

A few details:

- **Mail coss.com sends for you.** When Forms, Sign, Verify or Scheduling send from coss.com's own
  sender because you haven't set one, those emails get the same rules: no screening, no stored
  content, required TLS and hidden from staff.
- **Dropped email.** coss.com may not hear that a message was dropped for lack of TLS, so its
  recipient can stay `sent`.
- **Dedicated IPs.** Your mail uses shared IPs until your dedicated IPs require TLS, usually within
  minutes of turning the mode on.

## What it refuses

A request the mode refuses fails with `403` `permission_error` and the code
`hipaa_mode_restriction`, whatever the key's permissions. The [SMTP relay](/docs/email/smtp) answers
`550`. Retrying won't help, and neither will another key.

| Request in a live environment                                    | Result                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A send or broadcast that turns on `tracking`, or changing the tracking settings to turn it on | Refused. Without asking for it, emails simply go untracked.                           |
| Setting [content storage](/docs/email/content-storage) to `store` | Refused                                                                               |
| `coss.sms.send()` to a number outside the US                     | Refused. A message accepted before the mode was turned on fails with that code at dispatch. |
| `coss.verify.start()` for SMS without an `sms_from` in Verify's settings, or to a number outside the US | Refused. See [SMS codes](/docs/verify/sms#which-number-sends-it).                     |
| `coss.risk.checkPhone()`                                         | Refused                                                                               |
| `coss.risk.classify()`                                           | Refused                                                                               |

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
const { error } = await coss.sms.send({
  from: "+14155550123",
  to: "+447700900123",
  body: "Your appointment is confirmed.",
});
if (error?.code === "hipaa_mode_restriction") {
  // Not retryable: reach this recipient another way.
}
```

Other things stop quietly instead. `coss.risk.assess()` answers without content scores, and Forms and
Scheduling skip their content checks. Scheduling skips SMS reminders to numbers it can't text.

## What stays up to you

- Only send PHI once your BAA is in place, and only from live environments. Sandbox environments keep
  test emails and aren't covered by the controls above.
- Discarding only covers sent email and SMS. Received email and SMS, broadcasts, contacts, form
  submissions and bookings stay stored under your BAA for their normal
  [retention periods](/docs/data-retention).
- Keep PHI out of email subject lines and metadata such as tags and categories. Discarding removes a
  subject only once the email is finished, and tags and categories are kept.
- Webhook endpoints and log drains deliver to services you choose, so you need your own BAA with them
  where they receive PHI. Form submission webhooks include what was submitted.
- Calendars connected to Scheduling (Google, Microsoft, CalDAV) and Zoom receive event titles, under
  your own agreements with those providers.
- A captcha you configure on a form sends the submitter's address to that captcha provider.

## Vendors

| Vendor                          | Used for                                                  | In HIPAA mode                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Amazon Web Services             | Hosting, database, file storage and email delivery (SES)  | Under BAA                                                                     |
| Twilio Programmable Messaging   | SMS delivery                                              | Under BAA, for US numbers only, which is what Twilio's BAA covers             |
| Google Workspace                | Mail sent to support@coss.com                             | Under BAA                                                                     |
| Vercel AI Gateway               | AI content screening and classification                   | Not used                                                                      |
| Twilio Lookup                   | Phone number checks                                       | Not used                                                                      |
| Apple Push Notification service | Notifications to the iOS app                              | Not used                                                                      |
| Stripe                          | Payments                                                  | No PHI expected: it receives billing contacts and invoices only               |
| Sentry                          | Error reports                                             | No BAA. Content, email addresses and phone numbers are removed from reports, but error text can't be guaranteed free of PHI. |
