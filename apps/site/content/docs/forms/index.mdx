Forms gives each form on your site an endpoint to post to. You don't need a backend: a plain HTML
`<form>` posts to it, Risk screens every submission, and accepted ones go to your
[webhooks](/docs/webhooks), to your team by email and into your [contacts](/docs/email/contacts).
It uses the same API key, logs and sandbox environments as everything else.

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
const { data: form } = await coss.forms.create({
  name: "Contact us",
  allowed_origins: ["https://www.example.com"],
  notifications: { recipients: ["support@example.com"] },
});
// form?.endpoint: https://…/f/form_01k…
```

Then point your form at its `endpoint`:

```html
<form action="https://…/f/form_01k…" method="post">
  <input type="email" name="email" required>
  <textarea name="message"></textarea>
  <!-- The honeypot: people leave it empty, bots fill it in. -->
  <input type="text" name="_gotcha" style="display:none" tabindex="-1" autocomplete="off">
  <button>Send</button>
</form>
```

You can also create forms in the dashboard: each environment's **Forms** page shows the snippet to
paste.

## What happens to a submission

1. **The form checks it.** The form must be enabled and the post must come from one of its
   `allowed_origins` (if it lists any). With `fields` defined, every value is validated against
   them.
2. **Risk screens it.** The honeypot, then a [Risk assessment](/docs/forms/spam-filtering) of the
   submitter's email, IP, browser, captcha and the text they wrote. A submission Risk refuses is
   `spam`; one it wants reviewed is `held`.
3. **It is delivered.** An accepted submission sends `form.submission.received` to your
   webhooks, emails the form's recipients and adds the submitter to your contacts
   ([destinations](/docs/forms/destinations)). With `submitter.verify`, it first waits until the
   submitter clicks the link emailed to them ([double opt-in](/docs/forms/double-opt-in)).

Spam is answered exactly like a success, so nothing tells a bot what tripped it. You see it on the
form's **Spam** tab and can release it (`coss.forms.submissions.release`).

| Status                 | Means                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `accepted`             | Delivered to webhooks, notifications and contacts          |
| `held`                 | Risk asked for a review: release it, or leave it           |
| `spam`                 | Refused by the honeypot, Risk or the content check         |
| `pending_confirmation` | Waiting for the submitter to click the link in their email |

Accepted submissions are kept for <RetentionPeriod of="form_submissions" />; spam, held and
unconfirmed ones for <RetentionPeriod of="form_spam" />.
