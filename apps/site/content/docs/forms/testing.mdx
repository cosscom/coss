---
title: Testing
description: Try forms in a sandbox environment, and make Risk flag submissions on purpose.
---

Forms made with a sandbox (`coss_test_…`) key work end to end: posts are screened, stored and
delivered. What's different:

- **No emails reach anyone.** Notifications and verification emails go through the sandbox's
  fake provider and keep their content (live environments don't): open one in the dashboard's
  emails page and follow its link to confirm a submission. Contacts' double opt-in confirmations
  are sent the same way but keep no content even in a sandbox, so test those in a live
  environment with your own address.
- **`allowed_origins` and `redirect_url` can use `http://localhost`** while you develop.
- **Nothing is billed.**

Risk's [test inputs](/docs/risk/testing) work in submissions, so you can see each outcome:

| Submit                                    | Gets                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| A filled `_gotcha` field                  | `spam` (`honeypot`)                                     |
| Text with `[coss-test:spam]` in any field | `spam` (`content`): it scores 0.99                      |
| An email at `@disposable.test`            | A Risk reason (`email.disposable`): `held` or `spam`, per your thresholds |
| Captcha token `test-pass` / `test-fail`   | A passing or failing captcha (in `cf-turnstile-response`) |

Webhooks (`form.submission.*`) are delivered as in live environments, and
`coss trigger form.submission.received` sends a sample one.
