---
title: Double opt-in
description: Have submitters confirm their email address, for a contact or for the whole submission.
---

Forms has two kinds of double opt-in, which you can combine.

## Contacts

When a form [adds submitters to your contacts](/docs/forms/destinations#contacts), `contacts.double_opt_in`
decides whether a new (or unsubscribed) contact must confirm before they are `subscribed`. `null`
(the default) follows the environment's marketing settings (`double_opt_in_default`). A confirming
contact is `pending` and gets your [confirmation email](/docs/email/topics-and-preferences#double-opt-in),
the same as contacts you add with the API: so the marketing settings need a `confirmation_from`
sender first (saving the form checks). Without double opt-in, a new contact is `subscribed` and an
existing one keeps their status.

The submission itself is delivered at once (webhooks, notifications); only the contact waits.

## The whole submission

`submitter.verify` holds every submission until the submitter clicks the link coss.com emails them.
Until then it is `pending_confirmation`: no webhook, no notification, no contact.

```ts twoslash
import { Coss } from "coss";
const coss = new Coss();
// ---cut---
await coss.forms.create({
  name: "Beta access",
  submitter: { verify: true, confirmation_from: "beta@example.com" },
  redirect_url: "https://www.example.com/beta/confirmed",
});
```

- The form needs the submitter's address (`submitter.email_field`): a submission without one is
  refused.
- The email comes from `submitter.confirmation_from`, which in live environments must be on one of
  your verified domains: never from coss.com's address.
- The link (`…/f/c/<token>`) works for 7 days. Opening it shows a button; confirming sends the
  submission on (`form.submission.received`) and redirects to `redirect_url` if the form has one.
- Each address gets at most 3 verification emails a day across all of coss.com, and each
  organization 1,000. A submission never confirmed is deleted after
  <RetentionPeriod of="form_spam" />.

The submitter is told "Check your email" whatever happens to their submission, spam included.
