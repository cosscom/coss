## Countries

Every organization starts with live SMS allowed to the **United States and Canada**. Owners and
admins allow other countries on the dashboard's organization **SMS** page; a send to a country
that isn't allowed is refused with `422` `country_not_allowed` before anything is sent. Allow only
the countries you need: traffic to the others can't be pumped through your account.

- A few countries where SMS pumping fraud (fake sign-ups that send codes to numbers whose
  carriers pay the attacker) is common can only be allowed once coss.com support has unlocked
  them for you. Ask at [support@coss.com](mailto:support@coss.com) with your use case.
- SMS can only go to countries with a price: the [pricing page](/pricing#sms) lists every one.
- Toll-free numbers only send to the US and Canada, whatever your organization allows.

Sandbox environments can send to any number: nothing leaves coss.com.

## Pricing

Live SMS is available on paid plans and is paid for like any other usage: your plan's monthly
credit covers it first ([plans and billing](/docs/billing)). Free organizations can send in
sandbox environments only.

- **Sent messages** are priced per segment by the destination country, and billed once the
  message reaches a carrier (`sent`, `delivered` or `undelivered`). Failed, suppressed and
  canceled messages cost nothing. See [segments](/docs/sms/sending#segments) for how a message is
  counted.
- **Received messages** are priced per segment by the country of the number that received them.
  Replies dropped because their number has no receiving environment aren't billed.
- **Numbers** are billed monthly from the day you buy one until you release it.
- **US carrier registration fees** (A2P 10DLC brands and campaigns) are passed through at cost
  ([when each is charged](/docs/sms/senders#fees)).
