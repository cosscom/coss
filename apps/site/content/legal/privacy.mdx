This Privacy Policy explains how **Cal.com, Inc.** ("**Cal.com**", "**we**", "**us**") handles personal data in
connection with coss.com: the website and documentation at coss.com, the dashboard, the API and the SDK (together, the
"**Services**"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run coss.com: your account details, the data your organization sends through the API, and
  the technical data needed to keep the Services secure.
- For the messages you send through coss.com, **you** decide what is sent and to whom. We process that data on your
  behalf, as your processor.
- We automatically screen a sample of live messages for spam, phishing, malware and fraud, including with AI models.
  The AI provider retains nothing.
- We don't sell or share personal data for advertising, and coss.com runs no third-party analytics or advertising
  scripts.
- Cal.com participates in the **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** and its **UK Extension**.
- Questions? Write to [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com).

## 1. Who's who: controller and processor

**Cal.com as controller.** We decide how and why personal data is processed, and are responsible for it as controller
(or "business" under California law), for:

- your account and the members of your organization;
- billing and the business relationship with you;
- security, abuse prevention and the reputation of the Services;
- visits to coss.com and our communications with you.

**Cal.com as processor.** When you use the API or dashboard to send messages, the personal data in them is **Customer
Data**: message content, attachments, recipient addresses and phone numbers, delivery events, suppression lists and
the like. Your
organization is the controller of Customer Data, and we process it only on its instructions, under the Terms and our
[Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) (DPA). If you received a message sent through coss.com and want to exercise your privacy rights,
please contact the sender: they control that data. If you contact us instead, we will forward your request to them
where we can, and help them respond.

## 2. What we collect

**Account and organization data.** Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), two-factor
authentication settings and backup codes, passkey public keys, and, if your organization uses single sign-on, the
identity your identity provider returns. We also store organizations, projects, environments, memberships, roles and
invitations. If you subscribe to a paid plan, we store billing contact details; payment card details are handled by our
payment processor and never stored by us. If your organization sends SMS, we store the business details it registers
with mobile carriers (legal name, company type, tax ID, address, website, a description of its messages and how people
opt in to them, and an authorized contact's name, title, email address and phone number) and submit them, through our
SMS provider, to the carriers and their registries for review.

**Sessions and security data.** For each sign-in session, we store the IP address and browser user agent, so that you
can review and revoke your sessions. We use IP addresses and hashed email addresses to rate-limit sign-in attempts and
API requests. Platform administrators' actions are recorded in an audit log.

**API keys.** API keys are stored only as hashes, together with their environment, permissions and when they were last
used.

**Customer Data (processed on your behalf).**

- **Messages:** sender, recipients (to, cc, bcc), reply-to, subject, HTML and text content, headers, tags, category
  and scheduling details. Attachment contents are stored only until the message is sent, canceled or fails; after that
  we keep only the attachment's name, type and size.
- **Delivery events:** whether each recipient's message was delivered, bounced, complained about, suppressed or
  failed, with details from the receiving mail server.
- **Text messages (SMS):** the sending and receiving phone numbers, the text, tags and scheduling details, delivery
  events reported by mobile carriers (including their error codes), and the messages people send to your
  organization's phone numbers (their phone number and text, including opt-out requests such as STOP).
- **Open and click tracking:** only when you turn it on. When a recipient opens a tracked message or clicks a tracked
  link, we record the time, the link and the user agent of the request. We do not store their IP address.
- **Suppression lists:** addresses and phone numbers not to send to, added after permanent bounces, complaints,
  unsubscribes, SMS opt-outs or numbers that can't receive SMS, or by you.
- **Risk assessments:** when you ask us to assess an event in your own service (a sign-up, a login…), the email
  address, IP address and user agent you send for the person, your id for them, and what we derive from these (whether
  the email domain is disposable or can receive mail, the IP address's network and country and whether it is a Tor exit
  or a hosting network, and counts of recent events). To recognize the same person, network or mailbox in later
  assessments of the same environment, we keep only keyed hashes of the email address, IP address, network and your
  user id, and the countries and networks each user logged in from (by keyed hash of the user id). We look
  IP addresses up in data we download and host ourselves; we don't send them to anyone. To tell how old an email
  domain is, we ask its registry over RDAP, sending only the domain name. If you configure a captcha provider
  (Cloudflare Turnstile or hCaptcha), we send it the captcha token, your secret key and the person's IP address
  to verify the token. If you send text the person wrote (a profile bio, a message) with an assessment or to be
  classified, AI models score it through Vercel's AI Gateway, with zero data retention; we keep only the scores, never
  the text. For a payment, you may send the amount and currency, the payment card's issuing country and funding type,
  the billing and shipping countries, and your payment processor's fingerprint of the card (never the card number),
  which we keep only as a keyed hash. If you use our browser library (`coss/device`), it stores a random device
  identifier in the person's browser (local storage, no cookies) and reports it with the browser's time zone,
  languages, platform, screen size, touch support and whether the browser is automated; we keep the identifier only as
  a keyed hash.
- **Rate limiting for your service:** the keys you check against your own rate limit buckets (for example a user's
  email address) are kept only as keyed hashes, in our cache, until their bucket refills.
- **Domains and webhooks:** the sending domains you verify and their DNS records; your webhook endpoint URLs and
  signing secrets (encrypted at rest), and a record of each delivery attempt, including up to 1 KB of your endpoint's
  response.

**Website and documentation.** coss.com loads no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Our servers
and content delivery network process technical data such as IP addresses, request URLs and user agents to serve and
protect the site, and keep it in short-lived logs.

**Communications.** If you contact us, we keep your message and our reply. We send service emails, such as
verification, security, and reputation warnings and suspension notices to organization owners and administrators. These
are part of the Services and you can't opt out of them while you have an account.

## 4. Automated content screening

To protect the people who receive messages, and the deliverability of every customer, we automatically screen a sample
of messages sent from **live** environments before delivery. Sandbox messages are never screened.

- **What is screened:** a sample of messages per organization, more frequently for new senders. We never tell
  customers which messages will be sampled.
- **What the model sees:** the sender address, the subject, up to 8,000 characters of the message's readable text, and
  the host and path of its links (query strings removed); for SMS, the sending number, the text and the host and path
  of its links. It does not see recipient addresses or phone numbers, or attachments.
- **Who processes it:** the classification runs through Vercel's AI Gateway on AI models from its providers, with
  zero data retention: they may not keep the content or use it to train models.
- **What we keep:** the verdict and its scores, never the message content.
- **What can happen:** a message judged highly likely to be spam, phishing, malware or fraud is not delivered and its
  organization's live sending is suspended. Lower-confidence results can trigger a review of the organization's
  sending reputation.

A suspension never lifts by itself: a member of our staff reviews it. If you believe a decision is wrong, write to
[legal@cal.com](mailto:legal@cal.com) to have a person review it and to explain your point of view.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as described here:

- **Subprocessors** that help us run the Services, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing their
  services to us:
  - **Amazon Web Services, Inc.** (United States): hosting, databases, caching, queues and email delivery (Amazon
    SES).
  - **Vercel, Inc.** (United States) and the model providers available through its AI Gateway: AI content screening
    and the classification of content submitted for risk assessment, with zero data retention.
  - **Twilio Inc.** (United States): SMS delivery and receiving, phone numbers, and carrier registration of the
    business details organizations submit to send SMS.
  - **Cloudflare, Inc.** (United States): DNS for coss.com.

  The complete, current list of Cal.com subprocessors is in our Trust Center at
  [trust.cal.com](https://trust.cal.com). We give customers 30 days' notice of new subprocessors of Customer Data, and
  they may object on reasonable data protection grounds, as described in the [DPA](/legal/dpa#5-subprocessors).
- **Message delivery.** Sending a message necessarily discloses its content to the recipients' mail servers or, for
  SMS, to the recipients' mobile carriers. To let an organization send SMS in the United States, its registration
  details go to mobile carriers and their registries.
- **Your webhook endpoints** receive event data about your messages (never message bodies, headers, cc or bcc).
- **Your organization.** Organization owners and administrators can see the members of the organization and the data
  in it.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose data if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or to protect the
  rights, property or safety of Cal.com, our customers or others, including responding to abuse reports. Where
  permitted, we will tell the affected customer before disclosing Customer Data.
- **Business transfers.** If Cal.com is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may be
  transferred as part of it, subject to this policy.

**We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Account and organization data:** while your account is active. You can ask us to close your account at
  [support@cal.com](mailto:support@cal.com). After it is closed, you have at least 30 days to
  export your data, and we then delete it on our standard schedule, except what we must keep by law.
- **Messages and delivery events:** while your organization's account is active, so that you can look them up in the
  dashboard and the API. Your organization can ask us to delete them sooner by writing to
  [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com). Attachment contents are deleted automatically once a message is sent,
  canceled or fails.
- **Suppression lists, domains and webhook endpoints:** until your organization removes them, or its account is
  closed.
- **Sessions:** until they expire or are revoked.
- **Risk assessments:** for 1 year; the email address, IP address and user agent are deleted after 90 days. The keyed
  hashes used to recognize them (and cards and browsers) are deleted a year after they were last seen (IP addresses reported as fraud stop
  counting after 30 days). When your organization tells us one of its users is deleted, we delete that person's data
  from its assessments right away.
- **Content screening verdicts and reputation history:** for as long as the organization exists, so that past
  decisions can be reviewed.
- **Invoices and tax records:** as long as tax law requires, typically 7 to 10 years.
- **Logs:** short-lived, then deleted. Backups roll over on a fixed schedule.

## 9. Cookies

The coss.com dashboard uses only cookies that are strictly necessary: to keep you signed in, to complete two-factor
authentication and single sign-on, and to protect against attacks. The marketing site and documentation set no cookies
of their own, other than these when you are signed in, and we use no third-party advertising or analytics cookies. The
site may store display preferences, such as light or dark mode, in your browser's local storage. Because we use no
cookies that need consent, there is no cookie banner. We honor Global Privacy Control signals; since we neither sell
nor share personal data, there is nothing further for them to switch off.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data;
- object to, or restrict, our processing of it, including processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
- not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you, and to obtain
  human review of such a decision.

Some of these you can exercise yourself in the dashboard: you can update your name and review and revoke your sessions,
and your organization can manage its suppression list, domains and webhook endpoints. For anything else, write to
[privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com). We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and will
respond within the time the law requires (generally one month in the EEA and UK, 45 days in California). We will not
discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

**EEA, UK and Switzerland.** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We'd
appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

**California.** California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to
delete and correct it; and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do neither), and to limit the use of sensitive personal
information (we use it only as permitted for providing the Services and security). In the last 12 months we have
collected the categories described in [section 2](#2-what-we-collect): identifiers, customer records, commercial
information, internet or network activity, and account credentials, for the purposes in
[section 3](#3-why-we-process-it-legal-bases), and disclosed them only to the recipients in
[section 5](#5-who-we-share-it-with). You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

## 11. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in
transit and at rest, encryption of webhook signing secrets with AES-256-GCM, hashed passwords and API keys,
least-privilege access for our staff, audit logging of administrative actions, and support for two-factor
authentication, passkeys and single sign-on. No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please
report it as described at [cal.com/security](https://cal.com/security).

## 12. Children

The Services are for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from
anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
