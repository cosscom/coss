A mock product: a sidebar with four routes, "Calendar" active, and the
calendar filling the rest. The header is the product's — its title, ‹ ›
Today, a view switcher, Share and an avatar — but every control in it reads
the calendar it sits inside. Step the range and the heading changes.

## How it works

The shell is a flex row with a real height: `flex h-136`. The sidebar is a
fixed `w-44` that only appears once the frame is wide enough (`hidden
@2xl:flex` — a container query, so it follows the box, not the window), and
the calendar's cell is `min-w-0 flex-1`, so the row, not its content, decides
its size. `<Kloq>` — which is `h-full` and never reaches for the window —
fills it. That is the whole of
"[the container is the contract](/kloq/docs/quick-start#the-container-is-the-contract)":
give the component a box with a height and it renders into it.

`<Kloq>` renders no app bar of its own. `Header` is passed as `children`, so
it lands above the surface inside the calendar's provider, and it calls
`useKloq()` for everything it shows: `Nav` (in `parts.tsx`) reads `view`
for the title via `formatViewTitle` and `step` / `goToday` / `atToday` for
the range controls; the segmented control reads `viewSetting` and calls
`switchView`. `Kloq.Toolbar` is only the
bordered row. See [toolbar & chrome](/kloq/docs/chrome).

The calendar measures its own cell, not the window. At full width the cell
is about 730px: the stock
[responsive collapse](/kloq/docs/views#the-responsive-collapse) leaves three days
alone there and turns a seven-column week into three. Narrow the frame until
the sidebar drops away and the cell falls under 640px, where the collapse
shows a single day. The switcher disables what does not fit with
`k.fits(view)`, the same call the built-in switcher makes. For an app column
that is narrower by design, `responsive={{ breakpoint }}` moves that tier.

Nothing in the sidebar knows about the calendar. It is plain markup outside
`<Kloq>`, which is where product chrome that does not need calendar state
belongs.

## Take it further

Move `view` and `date` into your router and pass them as controlled props —
[View and date in the URL](/kloq/docs/examples/url-state) does exactly that — and
the header keeps working unchanged, because `useKloq()` reports the effective
state either way.
