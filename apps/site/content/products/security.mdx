<Section title="Sign-in your IT team can manage" lede="Organizations decide how their members sign in, and coss.com enforces it on every page of the dashboard.">

<Features>

<Feature title="Single sign-on">
Connect one OIDC or SAML identity provider for addresses on your verified domains. Owners can require SSO: members whose session didn't come from your provider are sent to sign in through it. Available on <PlansWith feature="sso" />. [Single sign-on](/docs/sso).
</Feature>

<Feature title="Directory sync">
SCIM adds and removes members as your directory changes and maps groups to the admin or member role. Deactivating a user removes them and ends their SSO sessions; owners are never changed. Available on <PlansWith feature="scim" />. [Directory sync](/docs/scim).
</Feature>

<Feature title="Two-factor and passkeys">
Members protect password sign-in with an authenticator app and backup codes, or sign in with a passkey that verifies the user. An organization can require two-factor for everyone, and sign-in forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. [Two-factor](/docs/two-factor).
</Feature>

<Feature title="Roles">
Members are owners, admins or members. Only owners change billing and require SSO; admins manage everything else. Members are read-only in the dashboard and create personal API keys where an owner or admin allows them. [Members and roles](/docs/members-and-roles).
</Feature>

<Feature title="Scoped API keys">
Keys are bound to one environment with full, sending, read-only or custom permissions. Personal keys stop working when their member leaves, including when the directory removes them. [API keys](/docs/environments#api-keys).
</Feature>

<Feature title="An audit log">
Every change by a member, an API key or coss.com support, with its target, environment and request id. Filter and export it, read it with `coss.auditEvents.list()`, or stream it with a log drain. Available on <PlansWith feature="auditLog" />. [Audit log](/docs/audit-log).
</Feature>

</Features>

</Section>

<Section title="Protecting every sender" lede="Abuse by one sender hurts the deliverability of all of them. coss.com watches for it before mailbox providers and carriers do.">

**Reputation guard.** Each organization has a standing, `good`, `warning` or `suspended`, measured from the bounce and complaint rates of its live email (and its SMS opt-outs and filtering) over a rolling window, with thresholds stricter than Amazon SES's. Owners and admins are emailed at a warning, and `account.*` webhooks tell your systems. A suspension stops live sending only, and is never lifted automatically: a person reviews it. [Reputation](/docs/reputation).

**Content screening.** A sample of live emails and text messages is screened for spam, phishing, malware and fraud right before it is sent. Which messages are sampled can't be predicted, a sample that can't be screened is held rather than sent unscreened, and verdicts never store the content. [Content screening](/docs/content-screening).

**Risk for your own product.** The same signals protect your sign-ups and logins: [Risk](/products/risk) scores them and answers allow, review or block.

</Section>

<Section title="Data handling" lede="What coss.com keeps, for how long, and how it is protected.">

Every kind of data has a published retention period, enforced by the worker's purges and listed on the [data retention](/docs/data-retention) page. A few of them:

- **Encryption**: data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest; webhook signing secrets and SMS provider credentials are also encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and passwords and API keys are stored only as hashes.
- **Less to keep**: an environment can [discard email content](/docs/email/content-storage) once it is sent. Risk remembers identifiers only as keyed hashes, and rate limit keys are stored hashed.
- **Clean logs**: request logs never keep the `Authorization` header, secrets or attachment bytes, and error reports are scrubbed of bodies, IP addresses, cookies and query strings.
- **Isolation**: every query is scoped to its organization, API keys are bound to one environment, and outbound webhooks reach public addresses only.

<Section title="Legal" lede="The documents your security review asks for, published.">

The [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) is part of the [Terms](/legal/terms): it covers the security measures above, the subprocessors and their locations, and transfers under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and the Acceptable Use Policy say what coss.com collects and what it allows. Vulnerabilities can be reported through the responsible disclosure program at [cal.com/security](https://cal.com/security).
